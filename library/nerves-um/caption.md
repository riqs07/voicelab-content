Myth or fact: "um" and "uh" are mistakes? 🤔

Linguists found they work like words: they tell listeners a short delay is coming. A few are normal, even useful. It's a stream of them that distracts.

The fix isn't to fight every one. Swap some for a one-second silent pause, especially right before your main point.
