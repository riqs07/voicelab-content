# The most confident sound? Silence. (Pause power)

The most confident sound in speaking? Silence.

Rushed: three “ums” in one sentence. Calm: the same news with one pause, and it sounds like you meant it.

Next time you feel an “um” coming, stop for a second instead. Nobody minds the pause. Everyone hears the “um”.
