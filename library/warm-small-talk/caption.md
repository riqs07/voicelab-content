# Myth: small talk makes you less happy

```
Myth or fact: small talk makes you less happy? 🤔

A study of 486 people found no link between small talk and being less happy. Having some substantive conversations still went with being more satisfied with life.

Small talk isn't the problem. Stopping there is. Start small, then ask one question that invites a real answer.
```
