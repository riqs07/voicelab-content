# Why you sound unsure (Sound check)

```
Do you sound unsure without meaning to?

When your voice goes up at the end of a statement, it sounds like a question, even when you're sure. Let the last word fall and it lands.

Record yourself saying one sentence about your day. Did the end go up or down?
```
