# Myth: reading makes you better at conversation

```
Myth or fact: reading about conversation skills makes you better at them? 🤔

In a week-long study, students who did small "talk to a stranger" missions became less worried about rejection and more confident. Students who only watched strangers did not change.

Training studies agree: practice with feedback changes behaviour; reading alone does not.

Watching didn't help. Doing did. What's one conversation you'll try this week? 👇
```
